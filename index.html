About

I'm a high-school student who spends a questionable amount of time building robots, writing software, and figuring out why the thing I just built isn't working.

My main home is FIRST Robotics. I've worked across FRC and FTC, moving between mechanical design, programming, driving, scouting, strategy, outreach, and team leadership. I enjoy the engineering itself, but I especially like building systems that help an entire team work better.

Outside robotics, I am heavily involved in 4-H, where I've held club leadership positions ranging from treasurer to president. I've also built physical projects for our club's outreach booths, including a flyer dispensing box and an animatronic display.

I also enjoy software and electronics projects, web development, competition strategy, music, and finding excuses to automate things that probably could have stayed manual.
