About

I'm a high-school student who spends a questionable amount of time building robots, writing software, and figuring out why the thing I just built isn't working.

My main home is FIRST Robotics. I've worked across FRC and FTC, moving between mechanical design, programming, scouting, strategy, outreach, and team leadership. I enjoy the engineering itself, but I especially like building systems that help an entire team work better.

Outside robotics, I work on software and electronics projects, compete in 4-H, experiment with web development, and generally find excuses to automate things that probably could have stayed manual.
